About

The Battle of Niså ("Slaget vid Nissan" in Swedish) was a naval battle fought on 9 August 1062 between the forces of Norwegian king Harald Hardrada and king Sweyn II of Denmark.

Read more at Wikipedia

With this tabletop miniatures game we're trying to recreate the battle itself, how it could have looked, and also if it could have ended in another way. The game includes a one-of-a-kind model of the coastal area outside of present-day city of Halmstad. It has been developed with support from Region Halland.

We're currently planning to Kickstart a boxed version of the game — follow the pre-launch page here!
